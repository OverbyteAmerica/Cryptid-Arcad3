CARTER'S URGENT CARE

CASE STUDY 02 — LAYERED DEFENSE: BEYOND THE FIREWALL

Incident Overview

Carter's Urgent Care relied solely on a firewall — creating a single point of failure with zero visibility into insider threats, lateral movement, or zero-day attacks. The organization processes sensitive PHI daily while facing phishing, unauthorized access attempts, and abnormal network traffic. A layered IDS/IPS strategy is the prescribed solution.

MITRE ATT&CK Mapping

Initial Access

Phishing / Social Engineering (T1566) — Attackers targeted staff via email and social media schemes, mirroring the AFLAC 2025 breach methodology.

Lateral Movement

Internal Credential Abuse & Pass-the-Hash (T1021) — Once inside, attackers move between systems using stolen credentials. Without NIDS, this goes undetected.

Privilege Escalation

Exploitation for Privilege Escalation (T1068) — Host-based HIDS detects unauthorized file changes and privilege escalation on employee machines.

Collection & Exfiltration

Automated Data Collection (T1119) / Exfiltration Over Network (T1048) — NIPS active blocking halts data exfiltration before PHI leaves the network.

Recommended Solution

Palo Alto Networks — Best Overall

Industry-leading threat prevention with strong medical device visibility and native firewall integration. Estimated deployment: $5,000–$15,000 upfront + $3,000–$8,000/year ongoing. Subscription-based IDS/IPS licensing scales with the organization.

Fortinet — Best Cost-Effective Option

~$5,000–$30,000 total. Easiest deployment of the major vendors. Strong fit for a budget-conscious mid-sized urgent care expanding to multiple locations.

IDS vs IPS — The Eyes & Hands Model

IDS = Eyes — Passive listener. Detects and alerts on suspicious traffic. Feeds the SIEM.
IPS = Hands — Active inline blocker. Stops ransomware and exfiltration in real time.

Pay Now vs Pay Later

The upfront cost of an IDS/IPS solution is measured in thousands. The average cost of a healthcare data breach in 2024 exceeded $10 million. HIPAA and PCI-DSS compliance penalties add further exposure. The math is clear.

Computer Forensics Value

IDS Logs
Document intrusion attempts with timestamps, source IPs, and attack signatures — admissible forensic evidence for legal proceedings.
IPS Logs
Record blocked attempts, enabling reconstruction of full attack timelines and identification of compromised systems.
SIEM Integration
Aggregates all alerts for post-incident investigation, stakeholder reporting, and regulatory compliance documentation.
